How to read it

Every number compares this period with the same number of days just before it.

Briefs sent is the number that matters most: someone filled in both steps of the form. It lands in your Gmail too.

Contact clicks are taps on Telegram or the email address. Intent, not a guaranteed message.

Started a brief means step one of the form (name and email) was filled. Compare it with briefs sent to see drop-off.

Lead sources is where the person first came from in that visit: Google, LinkedIn, Behance, or direct.

From buyer markets is the share of visits from countries you sell to. Higher means better-fit traffic.

Non-brand Google clicks leave out searches for "sorvel", so they show people who found you without knowing the name.

Mobile LCP is how long the biggest element takes to appear on phones. Green under 2.5 s, red over 4 s.

Behaviour follows one visit at a time: the pages in order, time on each, and whether it ended in a brief or a contact click. Blue cards are visits that became leads.

Read to 75% is the share of page views that scrolled at least three quarters down. Low numbers on long pages mean people stop early.

Left within 10 s is visits that saw one page for under ten seconds. Watch it by source: a high share means the ad, post or link promised something the page doesn't show.

Opening this dashboard leaves your browser out of all stats from then on (or open sorvel.agency/?me=1 on a phone; ?me=0 undoes it). Visits before that were counted.
