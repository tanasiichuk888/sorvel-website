Web design agency vs freelancer: which one to hire

Hiring for a website usually comes down to three choices: a freelancer, a large agency or a small senior studio. Each one fits a different situation.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 31 October 2026

In this article

The short answerSide by sideWhat to check before you hire anyoneWhere Sorvel fits

The short answer

Hire a freelancer for a small, clearly defined job on a tight budget. Hire a large agency when you need many specialists at once: research, content, paid media and development. Hire a small senior studio when you want agency-level design and build quality with one person accountable and a price that fits a startup.

Side by side

FreelancerSenior studioLarge agency
Typical website cost$1,000 to $8,000$5,000 to $20,000$30,000 and up
SpeedFast if availableFast, 3 to 6 weeksSlower, more meetings
Who does the workOne personSenior lead does the key workOften junior staff
Range of skillsUsually oneDesign, build, SEO basicsEverything
Main riskDisappears or gets overloadedLimited capacityCost and handovers

What to check before you hire anyone

  • Live work, not only mockups. Ask for links to sites they built that are online today.
  • Verified reviews. Reviews on Upwork, Fiverr or Clutch are harder to fake than quotes on a website.
  • Who does the work. Ask who designs and who builds your site day to day.
  • A written scope. Pages, features, revisions, timeline and price on paper before you start.
  • What happens after launch. Training, support and who owns the files and accounts.

Is a freelancer cheaper than an agency?

Usually yes, by half or more. The trade-off is capacity and range of skills, which matters more as the project grows.

What is a boutique web design studio?

A small team led by a senior designer or developer who does the key work personally, with agency-level process and a smaller overhead.

How do I know a web designer is reliable?

Look for live sites, verified platform reviews, a written scope and clear ownership of files and accounts.

How much should I budget for a business website?

For a custom site with a professional team, plan $5,000 to $20,000 depending on pages, CMS and content.
