Webflow vs Squarespace: which website builder is right for your business?

Squarespace is the easiest way to get a good-looking site online. Webflow is the tool you pick when the website has to grow, rank and convert. Here is how they compare in 2026, and how to choose.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 17 November 2026

The short answer

Choose Squarespace if you want to build and run a simple, attractive site yourself, quickly, from a template: a portfolio, a small local business, a personal brand or a small shop. Choose Webflow if the website is a growth channel for the business and needs custom design, a structured CMS, strong SEO control and a team that publishes often.

In price they are close for a basic site. The real difference is control: Squarespace limits what you can change, and Webflow lets a designer build almost anything.

Key takeaways

Squarespace wins on ease and all-in-one simplicity. Webflow wins on design freedom, CMS, SEO control, speed tuning and scaling. If you plan to hire a designer or run content marketing, Webflow is usually the better long-term home. If you will build the site yourself in a weekend, Squarespace is hard to beat.

Design and flexibility

Squarespace gives you polished templates made of sections you can rearrange and restyle. That keeps sites looking good, but it also means your site looks like a Squarespace site, and some layouts are simply not possible without code workarounds.

Webflow works like a visual front-end tool. A designer controls every element, spacing, breakpoint and interaction, so a custom Figma design can be built as designed. That freedom is why agencies and in-house teams use Webflow, and also why it takes longer to learn.

CMS, blogging and SEO

For a simple blog, both work. The gap opens when content becomes structured: case studies with filters, locations, team members, job listings, resources or multi-author blogs. Webflow's CMS lets you define collections with your own fields and design templates for each, so a new case study or location page is consistent and quick to publish.

On SEO, Squarespace covers the basics well: titles, descriptions, clean URLs, sitemaps and SSL. Webflow adds finer control: custom schema markup, per-collection SEO fields, 301 redirect management, canonical tags, clean semantic HTML and code embeds where needed. If organic search or AI search is a key channel, that control matters. Our GEO guide explains what AI search needs from a site.

Editing, teams and day-to-day work

In Squarespace, the person who builds the site usually also edits it, in the same simple editor. That is ideal for a business owner running their own site, but there is little protection against breaking a layout, and no real staging step for bigger changes.

Webflow separates the two jobs. Designers work in the Designer, where layout and styles live. Marketers and content editors work in the Editor, where they can change text, images and CMS items but cannot accidentally break the design. Larger changes can be prepared and published when ready, and roles and permissions control who can publish. For teams that publish weekly, this saves a lot of back-and-forth.

Speed, reliability and AI search

Both platforms host your site on a fast global network with SSL included, so uptime and security are handled for you. The difference is how much you can tune. In Webflow a designer controls exactly what loads on each page: image sizes and formats, fonts, scripts and animations. That makes it easier to hit good Core Web Vitals on mobile, which matters for Google rankings and for conversions.

AI search tools favour pages with clean structure, clear headings, tables and FAQ. Both platforms can produce that, but Webflow's custom code and schema options make it easier to add the structured data and page structure AI tools read best.

Ecommerce: which is better for selling online?

For a small shop that one person runs, Squarespace is simpler: products, inventory, payments and shipping are built into its commerce plans and easy to manage. Webflow ecommerce suits brands that want a fully custom shopping experience with a small catalogue, or a content-heavy site with a few products.

If selling online is the core of the business and the catalogue is large, neither is usually the best choice. Shopify is built for that; see our guide to Shopify store design.

Who should choose which

Pick Squarespace if:

  • You will build and update the site yourself without a designer
  • The site is a portfolio, local business site or personal brand with a few pages
  • You want everything (domain, email campaigns, scheduling, shop) in one simple dashboard
  • Budget for design is close to zero

Pick Webflow if:

  • The website brings in leads or sales and needs to convert
  • You want a custom design that does not look like a template
  • You publish content often or need structured CMS pages
  • SEO, AI search visibility and page speed are priorities
  • A marketing team needs to edit safely without breaking the design

Comparing other options too? See Webflow vs Wix, Webflow vs Framer and Webflow vs WordPress.

Mistakes to avoid when choosing

  • Choosing on the monthly plan price alone. A few dollars a month matters far less than whether the site brings in customers.
  • Picking Webflow and then building it yourself in a rush. Webflow rewards good structure. A messy first build is hard to maintain.
  • Picking Squarespace for a site you know will grow fast. If you plan dozens of landing pages, a resource library or several languages, you may outgrow it within a year.
  • Forgetting the content. On either platform, clear copy, real photos and a strong offer matter more than the builder.

Moving from Squarespace to Webflow

Squarespace does not export a complete site. Blog posts and some content can be exported, but design, layout and most page content are rebuilt in Webflow. The process looks like this:

  1. List every URL and your top pages in Google Search Console.
  2. Export what Squarespace allows and download your images.
  3. Design the new site in Figma and build it in Webflow, with blog posts moved into a CMS collection.
  4. Set 301 redirects for every URL that changes, especially blog URLs.
  5. Connect your domain, submit the sitemap, and watch Search Console for a few weeks.

Is Webflow better than Squarespace?

Webflow is better for custom design, structured content, SEO control and growing businesses. Squarespace is better for people who want to build a simple, attractive site themselves without a designer.

Is Webflow harder to use than Squarespace?

Yes, building in Webflow takes longer to learn. Once a designer has built the site, editing text, images and blog posts in Webflow's Editor is about as easy as in Squarespace.

Which is cheaper, Webflow or Squarespace?

Plans are similar: as of November 2026 Webflow starts at $15 a month on annual billing and Squarespace's Basic plan at about $16 to $19. The bigger cost difference is usually design and build.

Which is better for SEO, Webflow or Squarespace?

Both cover the basics. Webflow offers more control over schema, redirects, CMS SEO fields and clean code, which helps competitive search and AI search.

Can I move my Squarespace site to Webflow?

Yes, but the design is rebuilt rather than exported. Blog content can be moved into Webflow's CMS, and 301 redirects protect your rankings.

Is Squarespace good for ecommerce?

It is good for small shops run by one person. For large catalogues Shopify is usually a better fit, and Webflow suits small, design-led stores.

Should a startup use Webflow or Squarespace?

Most startups that plan to grow through content, SEO and paid campaigns are better served by Webflow, because the site can scale without a rebuild.
