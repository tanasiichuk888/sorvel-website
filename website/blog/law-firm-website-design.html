Law firm website design: what clients look for before they call

People looking for a lawyer are often stressed and in a hurry. They compare a few firms on their phone and call the one that feels most trustworthy and easiest to reach. A good law firm website is built for exactly that moment.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 1 November 2026

The short answer

A law firm website that brings in consultations shows who you help and where, proves experience early, and puts a way to call or book on every screen, especially on mobile. Practice area pages and local SEO bring most of the search traffic.

A custom law firm website usually costs $5,000 to $20,000. With our studio it starts from $6,900, and a single call-first landing page for ads starts from $2,400.

Key takeaways

Calm the visitor, then make calling easy. Give every practice area its own page. Show real attorneys and real credentials. Design for phones first, because that is where most legal searches happen.

Trust signals clients look for

  • Real photos of the attorneys with bar admissions, years of practice and education.
  • Results and reviews, presented within the advertising rules of your bar.
  • Languages spoken, if you serve more than one community.
  • Clear, calm language. No legal jargon in the headlines.
  • Free consultation details: how it works, how long it takes and that it is confidential.

Practice area pages: the backbone of the site

Each practice area deserves its own page. These pages rank for searches like "DUI lawyer in Houston" and give visitors confidence that you handle their exact situation. A strong practice area page includes:

  1. What the area covers, in plain words.
  2. Typical situations clients come with.
  3. How the process works and what happens next.
  4. Relevant results or experience.
  5. An FAQ with the questions clients ask on the first call.
  6. A call or booking button, repeated.

Call-first design on mobile

Most people search for a lawyer on their phone, often in a stressful moment. A sticky call button, click-to-call numbers and a short contact form matter more than any animation. For a Houston criminal defense firm we designed a bilingual landing page with proof above the fold and a way to call from every single section, built in Webflow.

Bilingual and multilingual law firm sites

If you serve Spanish-speaking or other communities, a real second-language version, not an automatic translation widget, builds far more trust. Plan for a language switcher, translated practice pages and staff who answer in that language.

Local SEO for law firms

  • A Google Business Profile with the same name, address and phone as the website.
  • Practice area pages that mention the city or region you serve.
  • Reviews on Google and legal directories.
  • Structured data for your firm, attorneys and locations.
  • Helpful articles that answer common legal questions in your area.

Common law firm website mistakes

  • Stock photos of gavels and handshakes instead of the real team.
  • One "Services" page for every practice area.
  • Phone numbers that are not clickable on mobile.
  • Long contact forms that ask for the whole story up front.
  • Slow pages, which hurt both rankings and patience.
