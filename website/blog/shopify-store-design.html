Shopify store design: what makes a store convert in 2026

Most Shopify stores start from the same themes, so they end up looking alike. The stores that sell more are designed around how their customers actually shop, page by page. Here is what that means in practice.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 1 November 2026

The short answer

A Shopify store converts when shoppers find the right product in a few taps, understand why it is worth the price and trust the store enough to buy. That comes down to clear navigation, strong product pages, visible trust signals and a smooth cart, all designed for mobile first.

Key takeaways

Design the product page first, because that is where the decision happens. Put reviews, delivery and returns next to the buy button. Most store traffic is mobile, so the phone layout is the main layout.

The pages that matter most

PageWhat it must doCommon mistake
HomepageSay what you sell and for whom, and route shoppers to the right collectionA slideshow of banners with no clear path
CollectionMake filtering, sorting and comparing easyToo many products with no filters on mobile
ProductShow the product well, answer questions, make variants clearSmall photos and key details hidden in tabs
CartRemove doubt with totals, delivery and returnsSurprise costs at the last step
About and FAQBuild trust in a brand people do not know yetGeneric copy with no real people or story

Designing a product page that sells

  • Large, real photos with zoom, plus at least one image that shows scale or the product in use.
  • A clear price and variants. Colour swatches beat dropdowns when colour matters.
  • Benefits before specs. Three short reasons to buy, then the details.
  • Delivery and returns next to the button, not only in the footer.
  • Reviews with photos close to the top.
  • A sticky add-to-cart button on mobile.

For a varsity jacket brand we linked 44 colourways with swatches on one product page, so shoppers could compare without leaving it.

Mobile first, not mobile later

On most stores the majority of visits come from phones. Design decisions should start there: thumb-friendly buttons, filters that open in a drawer, short forms and fast-loading images. A store that looks great on desktop and awkward on a phone loses the majority of its shoppers.

Trust signals that sell

  • Reviews and ratings, ideally with customer photos.
  • Clear delivery times, costs and returns.
  • Secure checkout and familiar payment options shown before checkout.
  • Real people behind the brand: founders, makers, the team.
  • Press mentions or partner logos if you have them.

Theme or custom design?

Theme with custom sectionsFully custom design
Typical costLowerHigher
Time1 to 3 weeks4 to 8 weeks
Brand fitGood, within the theme's limitsBuilt around your brand
Best forNew stores, small cataloguesBrands that need to stand out

Many stores do well with a strong theme plus custom-designed key pages: homepage, product page and collection. That keeps the cost down where it matters less.

How a Shopify design project works with us

  1. Brief. Your products, customers, competitors and stores you like.
  2. Structure. Navigation, collections and the homepage flow.
  3. Design in Figma. Homepage, collection, product, cart and content pages, for desktop and mobile.
  4. Build. A custom Shopify theme or custom sections in your current theme.
  5. Launch. Testing on real devices, speed checks and a handover.

Recent examples include a store for personalised children's books where the child is the hero, and a fashion store with 44 colourways.

Common Shopify design mistakes

  • Too many apps that slow the store down.
  • Pop-ups that cover the product on mobile.
  • Hiding shipping costs until checkout.
  • Stock photos instead of real product photography.
  • A homepage that tries to show every product at once.
