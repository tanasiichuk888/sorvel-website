How much a Webflow website costs in 2026

A Webflow website can cost a few hundred dollars or well over $30,000. The difference comes down to a handful of things you can see in advance, so here they are.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 31 October 2026

In this article

The short answerTypical price rangesWhat moves the priceCosts after launchHow to get an accurate quote

The short answer

A custom-designed Webflow website for a startup or growing business usually costs $5,000 to $20,000 with a professional team and takes 3 to 6 weeks. With our studio a website starts from $6,900, a single landing page from $2,400, and moving an existing site to Webflow from $2,900.

Rule of thumb

Price follows the number of unique page layouts, the CMS structure and how much custom motion you need. The number of pages matters much less.

What moves the price

  • Unique layouts. Home, service, pricing, case study and blog templates each take design and build time. Extra pages on an existing layout are cheap.
  • CMS structure. A blog is simple. Linked collections for case studies, integrations, team and resources take more planning.
  • Motion and interactions. Scroll animations and interactive product scenes add days, not hours.
  • Content. Ready copy and images keep the price down. Copywriting and imagery add to it.
  • Integrations and languages. CRM forms, analytics, gated content and extra locales all add scope.

Costs after launch

Webflow charges a monthly site plan for hosting and the CMS, and a workspace plan if your team needs editing seats. Plan prices change, so check Webflow's pricing page for current numbers. Beyond that there is no plugin licence or server maintenance to pay for, which is where Webflow usually saves money compared with WordPress. See Webflow vs WordPress.

How to get an accurate quote

  • List the page types you need, not just the page count.
  • Say what content you already have and what needs writing.
  • Share two or three websites you like and why.
  • Ask for a fixed price with written scope and timeline before work starts.

Is Webflow expensive?

The plan is a monthly fee, but there is no hosting, plugin or maintenance bill on top. Over a year, many teams spend less than on a WordPress site they keep patching.

How long does a Webflow website take?

Usually 3 to 6 weeks for 5 to 10 custom pages, depending on how ready the content is.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

Yes. Your team edits text, images and CMS items in the Webflow editor without touching the design.

Is a Webflow template good enough?

For testing an idea, yes. Once the site has to sell a real product, a custom design usually converts better and is easier to grow.
