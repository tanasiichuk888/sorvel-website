Wix to Webflow migration: how to move without losing traffic

There is no button that converts a Wix site into Webflow. Here is what you can export, what has to be rebuilt, how long it takes, and the steps that keep your Google rankings intact.

The short answer

A Wix to Webflow migration is a rebuild, not an export. Wix does not let you export your design, code or interactions, so the pages are recreated in Webflow. Some content can be moved with files: Wix CMS collections and store products can be exported as CSV, while blog posts, images and forms need manual work or a script.

Most small business sites take 2 to 6 weeks to migrate. The step that decides whether you keep your Google traffic is the redirect map: every old URL must point to its new page before you switch the domain.

Key takeaways

Plan the migration as a chance to fix the design, not copy it pixel by pixel. Export what Wix allows, move the rest with care, and keep URLs the same where you can. Add 301 redirects for every URL that changes, then watch Search Console for a few weeks after launch.

Why businesses move from Wix to Webflow

Wix is easy to start with. Businesses usually move when the site becomes important to sales and the limits start to show:

  • Design control. Webflow gives designers full control of layout, responsive breakpoints and animations.
  • Speed and clean code. Webflow outputs lean HTML and CSS, which helps Core Web Vitals.
  • CMS and content. Webflow's CMS handles structured content such as case studies, locations, team members and blog posts with custom fields.
  • SEO control. Per-page meta tags, structured data, clean URLs and redirects are all in your hands.
  • Team editing. Marketers can edit text and publish posts without breaking the layout.

What you can export from Wix, and what you can't

This is the part most people underestimate. Based on Webflow's own migration guidance and Wix's export options as of November 2026:

ItemCan it be exported?How it moves to Webflow
Design, layout, codeNoRebuilt in Webflow (often redesigned)
Animations and interactionsNoRecreated with Webflow Interactions
CMS collectionsYes, as CSVImported into Webflow CMS collections
Store productsPhysical products yes, digital products noCSV import into Webflow Ecommerce, or rebuilt
Blog postsNo direct exportCopied manually or moved with a script into a Blog collection
Images and mediaDownloaded one by one or in batchesUploaded to Webflow's asset manager, ideally as WebP
FontsNo (Wix font licences cover Wix only)Re-licensed or replaced, then uploaded
Forms and integrationsNoRebuilt with Webflow forms or connected tools

Your domain stays yours. If you bought it through Wix, you can keep it there and point the DNS records to Webflow, or transfer it to a registrar. Webflow itself is not a domain registrar.

Step-by-step: moving from Wix to Webflow

  1. Crawl the current site. List every URL, page title, meta description and heading. Export your top pages and queries from Google Search Console so you know what must not break.
  2. Export what Wix allows. CMS collections and products as CSV, plus every image and file you need.
  3. Plan the structure. Decide the new sitemap, keep proven URLs where possible, and design CMS collections for repeated content such as blog posts, services or locations.
  4. Design in Figma. Use the move to fix weak pages, simplify navigation and make calls to action clearer.
  5. Build in Webflow. Build components and a style system, then pages and CMS templates. Import CSV data and move blog posts.
  6. Recreate SEO details. Titles, meta descriptions, alt text, Open Graph images, canonical tags and structured data for every important page.
  7. Write the redirect map. Add a 301 redirect in Webflow for every old URL that changes. Wix blog URLs such as /post/your-title often change, so check them closely.
  8. Test on staging. Forms, mobile layouts, page speed, broken links and analytics events on the Webflow staging domain.
  9. Switch the domain. Lower your DNS TTL a day or two before, connect the domain in Webflow, publish, then update the DNS records.
  10. Monitor after launch. Submit the new sitemap in Search Console, watch for 404 errors and ranking changes for four to eight weeks, and fix any gaps quickly.

How to keep your Google rankings

Rankings drop after a migration when Google cannot match old pages to new ones, or when the new pages say less than the old ones did. To avoid that:

  • Redirect every changed URL with a 301 to the closest matching page, not to the home page.
  • Keep the content that ranks. Improve it, but do not delete sections that answer the queries you rank for.
  • Keep internal links working and update them to the new URLs.
  • Match or beat the old speed. Compress images and avoid heavy scripts.
  • Re-add tracking so you can compare traffic before and after.

A short, temporary wobble in rankings is normal for a few weeks. A lasting drop almost always points to missing redirects or missing content. The same rules apply to any platform move; our guide to migrating WordPress to Webflow without losing SEO goes deeper on redirects.

Before you start: the migration audit

A one-hour audit before any design work saves days later. Collect these first:

  • A full URL list from a crawler or your Wix sitemap, including blog posts, product pages and hidden pages that still get traffic.
  • Your top 20 pages by clicks in Google Search Console and by visits in analytics. These need the most care.
  • Every form and integration: booking tools, email marketing, chat, payment and CRM connections, plus who receives each form.
  • Apps you rely on. Wix apps for events, bookings, members or reviews need a Webflow-friendly replacement before launch.
  • Access details: who controls the domain, DNS and Google accounts, so the cutover is not blocked on launch day.

If your Wix site depends on Wix Bookings, members areas or a large Wix store, plan those replacements first. They usually take more time than the pages themselves.

Common Wix to Webflow migration mistakes

  • Switching the domain before redirects are live. Google finds broken URLs on day one and starts dropping them.
  • Redirecting everything to the home page. Google treats this like a missing page. Map each URL to its closest match.
  • Forgetting image alt text and meta descriptions. They do not move automatically and are easy to lose.
  • Copying the old design one to one. You pay for a rebuild anyway; fix what did not work.
  • Using Wix fonts without a licence. Fonts licensed for Wix cannot be uploaded to Webflow. Pick a replacement early so the design is built with it.
  • Not testing forms. A broken contact form after launch is the most expensive bug a small business site can have.

What changes for your team after the move

Day to day, your team edits text, images and CMS items in Webflow's Editor, and the layout stays protected from accidental changes. Blog posts, case studies or team members are added as CMS items with set fields, so every new page looks consistent. Larger layout changes go through a designer or someone trained in Webflow's Designer.

Ask for a short handover: how to edit pages, add posts, update SEO fields and publish. A good handover means your team is not dependent on a developer for small edits.

DIY or hire someone?

You can migrate a five-page site yourself if you are comfortable in Webflow and careful with redirects. Hire help when the site brings in leads or sales, has a blog with many posts, uses a store, or needs a redesign at the same time. The cost of a botched migration is lost traffic for months.

Can I export my Wix website to Webflow?

Not directly. Wix does not export design, code or interactions, so the site is rebuilt in Webflow. CMS collections and physical store products can be exported as CSV and imported.

How long does a Wix to Webflow migration take?

Most small business sites take 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the number of pages, blog posts, CMS items and whether you redesign at the same time.

Will I lose my SEO when moving from Wix to Webflow?

Not if you keep ranking content, set up 301 redirects for every changed URL before switching the domain, and monitor Search Console after launch. A short wobble is normal; a lasting drop usually means missing redirects.

How much does it cost to move from Wix to Webflow?

Sorvel migrations start at $2,900 for small sites. Larger sites with blogs or a full redesign get a fixed quote after a review; full websites start at $6,900.

Can I keep my domain if I bought it on Wix?

Yes. You can keep it registered with Wix and point the DNS records to Webflow, or transfer it to another registrar. Webflow does not sell domains.

How do I move my Wix blog posts to Webflow?

Wix blog posts cannot be exported directly, so they are copied manually or moved with a script into a Webflow Blog collection, keeping titles, dates, images and URLs where possible.

Is Webflow better than Wix?

Webflow is better for design control, speed, structured content and SEO control. Wix is simpler for owners who want to drag and drop without a designer.
