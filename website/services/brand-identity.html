How many logo options do we get?

We present two clear directions, then refine the one you choose. Fewer options, more thought behind each.

Can you refresh our brand instead of starting over?

Yes. Often a stronger type system, cleaner colours and better rules are enough.

Can you design the website at the same time?

Yes, and it's the best way to do it. The site then becomes the first real test of the brand.

Do we get the source files?

Yes. All files are yours, organised in your own Figma, with exports for every use.

Do you name companies?

Naming isn't part of our standard scope, but we can suggest options as part of the direction phase.
