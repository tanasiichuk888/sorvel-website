How much does mobile app design cost?

Mobile app UI/UX design with Sorvel starts at $4,800 for the core flows of a new app or a redesign, and usually takes 3 to 6 weeks. You get user flows, every screen for iOS and Android, a clickable Figma prototype and a design system your developers can build from.

Do you design for both iOS and Android?

Yes. We design one system that follows each platform's conventions where it matters, such as navigation, gestures and system controls.

Can you also build the app?

Yes. We design and develop mobile apps, or hand the design to your own developers with full specs.

Do we get the Figma files?

Yes. You own all files, components and the prototype from the start.

Can you redesign an existing app?

Yes. We start with a short UX review of the current app, then fix the flows that hurt retention and conversion first.

How long does mobile app design take?

Usually 3 to 6 weeks for the core flows of a new app, depending on the number of screens and feedback speed.
