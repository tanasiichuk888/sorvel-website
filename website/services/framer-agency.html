How much does a Framer website cost?

A custom Framer website with Sorvel starts at $10,500 for a typical 5 to 10 page site and takes 2 to 5 weeks. Single Framer landing pages start at $3,500. Framer's own hosting plan is paid separately.

Framer or Webflow?

Framer is faster to launch and great for motion-rich sites with simpler content. Webflow is stronger for large CMS sites and complex content. We recommend one in the plan.

Is Framer good for SEO?

Yes, when built properly: clean structure, meta tags, fast images and a sitemap. We set all of this up and can add schema markup.

Can our team edit the site?

Yes. Framer's editor lets your team change text, images and CMS items, and we hand over a short video guide.

Can you move our site to Framer?

Yes. We migrate content and set redirects so you keep your rankings.
