How much does web app development cost?

A first version of a web app with Sorvel starts at $17,500 and usually takes 6 to 12 weeks, covering discovery, UX and UI design and a production build. The final price depends on roles, integrations and how much needs to be built from scratch.

Usually React or Next.js on the front end with a proven back end and database, chosen for your product and team.

Yes. We review what you have and continue from there.

Do we own the code and designs?

Yes, from day one.

How do you keep the budget under control?

We agree a fixed scope for the first version and build in weekly steps you can see and test.

Do you support the app after launch?

Yes, through a monthly development plan or a block of hours.
