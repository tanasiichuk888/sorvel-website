Will we lose our Google rankings?

Not if it's done properly. We map every old URL to a new one, keep your meta and structure, and watch Search Console for 30 days after launch.

Can you migrate and redesign at once?

Yes, and it often saves time. We'd still keep URLs and content structure stable where rankings depend on them.

How many pages can you move?

From a 10 page site to thousands of blog posts. Larger content sets are imported automatically, not by hand.

Will the site be down during the move?

No. We build on the new platform in parallel and switch the domain only when everything is checked.

What about forms, integrations and analytics?

We list them all in the audit and reconnect each one on the new platform.
