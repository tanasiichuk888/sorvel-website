Why it works this way

Every decision on record.

Calls feel productive, but half of what's said is forgotten by Friday. Writing things down is slower for an hour and faster for the whole project.

You review designs when it suits you, not when a meeting is booked. Feedback lands right on the screen it's about. And when someone new joins your team, the full history is already there.

It also means you never wait for us. Updates arrive every 24 to 48 hours, whether you asked or not.

Step by step

From brief to live site.

A typical website takes 3 to 6 weeks. A landing page, 1 to 2. Here's what happens in between.

Day 0

You send a brief

A few lines through the form, by email or on Telegram. What you're building, what the site should do, any links or deadlines. No call needed.

  • Short brief form5 min
  • Links to examples you likeoptional
  • NDA first, if you need oneoptional

Within 24h

You get a written plan

We read everything, look at your current site and competitors, then reply with a clear plan: what we'll make, in what order, how long it takes and the fixed price.

  • Scope and page list
  • Timeline with milestones
  • Fixed price, split in two
  • Questions we need answered

Days 1 to 3

Kickoff

Once you approve, we set up a shared board and folder. You send content and access, we send a short questionnaire about your audience, voice and goals.

  • Shared board with every task
  • Brand, content and access checklist
  • Sitemap and messaging draft

Weeks 1 to 3

Design in Figma

We start with structure and copy, then the look. You review on your own time, leave comments right on the design and see changes within 24 to 48 hours.

  • Wireframes and copy structure
  • Desktop and mobile design
  • Interactive prototype
  • Two rounds of revisions per page

Weeks 2 to 5

Build

The approved design becomes a real site in Webflow, Framer or WordPress, with CMS, animations and forms. You get a private link and watch it come together.

  • Pixel-accurate build
  • CMS you can edit yourself
  • Animations and interactions
  • Speed, SEO and accessibility checks

Launch +30 days

Launch and aftercare

We test on real devices, connect your domain and go live. For 30 days after launch, fixes are on us. After that, you can stay on a monthly plan.

  • Cross-device QA
  • Domain, redirects and analytics
  • Video guide to edit your site
  • 30 days of free fixes

Do we ever need a call?

No. Everything works in writing. If you'd still prefer a quick call at some point, just say so, but most clients never need one.

How much of my time does it take?

About an hour at kickoff for content and access, then 15 to 30 minutes per review round. Everything else is on us.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief, your logo and brand files if you have them, and any content you already have. We help write or polish the rest.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds per page in design, plus fixes during the build. In practice most pages are approved after the first round.

What happens after the 30 days?

You can edit the site yourself in the CMS, order small tasks as needed, or stay on a monthly plan for ongoing work.
