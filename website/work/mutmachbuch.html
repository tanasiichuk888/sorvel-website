E-commerce · Personalised children's books · Germany

A bookshop where every child is the hero.

Mutmachbuch creates personalised children's books that build confidence, with the child as the main character. We designed a Shopify store that feels as warm and magical as the books, while making personalising and ordering a book quick and clear.

The challenge

Parents buy a feeling. The store has to sell it.

A personalised book is a gift with a purpose: helping a child feel brave and capable. The store needed to show that story, not just a product grid.

Personalisation also adds steps, so the path from choosing a book to ordering it had to stay simple.

  • Make the books the star
  • Explain personalisation in four steps
  • Show why the books help children grow

A gift, not a product

Parents need to see the emotional value, not just the price.

Personalisation feels complex

Choosing, personalising and adding a dedication had to feel easy.

Trust for a new brand

Reviews, real authors and 'no AI texts' needed to be visible.

Key decisions

What leads the store?

How is personalisation explained?

How do you build trust?
