Work/AWS Wrap

Automotive · Paint protection and wraps · South Florida

Pick your protection, see the price.

AWS Wrap is an XPEL Authorized Dealer in Hollywood, Florida, protecting and restyling cars for more than ten years. We designed and built their new WordPress site in one month: a bold look that matches the cars they work on, a price calculator people actually play with, and a page for every service South Florida drivers search for.

The challenge

People want a price. Shops hide it.

People shopping for paint protection film compare shops on two things: how the work looks and what it costs. Most local shops show a gallery and a "call for a quote" button, so visitors leave to find a number somewhere else.

AWS Wrap needed a site that looks as premium as the cars in their garage, gives a clear starting price up front, and shows up when someone in Miami, Hollywood or Fort Lauderdale searches for a specific service.

  • Starting prices visible before the first call
  • A look that matches high-end cars
  • A page for every service people search for

No price, no lead

Visitors comparing shops want a number. Without one, they leave to find it somewhere else.

A long list of services

PPF, tint, ceramic coating, color change, fleet wraps and more, each with its own buyers and questions.

Local search

People search by service and city. One generic services page can't rank for all of them.

How we worked

One month, from the first sketch to a live site.

A short timeline meant clear stages and quick feedback. Each stage ended with something AWS Wrap could see and approve.

Discovery

What drivers ask before buying

We went through the services, packages and prices with the team, looked at other South Florida shops, and listed what people search for.

  • Services and pricing review
  • Local competitor review
  • Search terms per service

Structure

Price and proof up front

A homepage that leads with the dealer status, then the calculator, reasons to trust, services, real work and plans.

  • Site map and page order
  • Calculator logic
  • Service page template

Design

Dark, bold and fast

Black backgrounds, a racing red accent and a wide techno typeface, built around the shop's own photography. Desktop and mobile.

  • Homepage and inner pages
  • Pricing and calculator UI
  • Mobile layouts

Build

WordPress, ready to grow

Developed in WordPress with the interactive calculator, editable service pages and a blog, then tested on real phones.

  • WordPress development
  • Calculator and forms
  • Blog and SEO basics

The idea

Let people price it themselves.

Instead of a "call for a quote" button, the homepage has a slider. Move it and the protected area on the car grows while the price updates, from full front to track pack to full body. People understand what they're paying for before they ask.

Try it. This is a working replica of the calculator.

Trust

Their own cars, not stock photos.

Four reasons to choose AWS Wrap sit next to a real car from their shop: ten years of experience, wrapping every detail including door jambs, friendly service and premium materials.

Services

One card, one service, one page.

Ceramic coating, window tint, paint protection film, color change, fleet wraps and more. Each card leads to its own page written for local search, like "XPEL Ceramic Coating in Hollywood, FL".

Portfolio

The cars do the selling.

A clean six-car grid on the homepage, all shot in the same location so the colors and finishes speak for themselves, with a link to the full portfolio.

Pricing

Three plans, no surprises.

Full front, full body and color change, each with a starting price and a short list of what's included. The most popular plan is marked, and every card ends with "Get Quote".

Social proof

Reviews from the neighbourhood.

Short customer reviews in a carousel, each signed with a first name and a South Florida area like Miami Lakes or Hialeah, so locals see people like them.

Content

A blog the team runs itself.

Articles on tint, PPF costs and fleet wraps answer the questions people type into Google. Built in WordPress, so the team publishes new posts without a developer.

Mobile

Big type, big buttons, same punch.

On a phone the headline keeps its weight, both actions go full width and the calculator stacks with a thumb-sized slider.

Key decisions

Choices that turn browsing into quote requests.

How do you talk about price?

The usual answer

"Call for a quote" and nothing else.

What we did

An interactive calculator on the homepage plus three plans with starting prices.

What photos do you show?

The usual answer

Stock images and manufacturer shots.

What we did

Their own cars, shot in one location, in the gallery and the "Why choose us" block.

How do you present services?

The usual answer

One page with a long list.

What we did

A card per service, each leading to its own page written for a city, like Hollywood or Miami.

How do you earn trust fast?

The usual answer

A row of brand logos.

What we did

"XPEL Authorized Dealer" in the headline and local reviews signed with a neighbourhood.
