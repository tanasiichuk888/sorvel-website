Work/NOIR Properties

Real estate · Costa Blanca and Costa del Sol · Spain

A Spanish property agency that feels personal.

NOIR Properties helps international buyers find homes on the Costa Blanca and Costa del Sol, with AI-powered property matching. We designed a website that pairs a confident, editorial look with fast search, real client stories and a direct line to the agents.

The challenge

Buying abroad is a leap. The site has to make it feel safe.

Most visitors are buying a home in another country, often for the first time. They need to trust the agency before they trust a listing, and they want to start searching straight away.

NOIR also had a story worth telling: a licensed agency with over ten years of local experience and an AI matching approach that sets it apart from listing portals.

  • Search on the first screen
  • Trust signals early on the page
  • A personal, human tone

Trust from a distance

Buyers abroad can't walk into the office, so licence, experience and real people had to be visible.

Search without friction

Area, budget and bedrooms needed to be one step away, not buried in a menu.

More than a portal

The agency's AI matching and personal service had to stand out from listing sites.

Key decisions

Small choices that shape the page.

What's on the first screen?

The usual answer

A big photo and a slogan.

What we did

The slogan plus a search bar for area, budget and bedrooms, so buyers can start straight away.

How do you show trust?

The usual answer

Logos and a generic promise.

What we did

Licence number, years of experience, a named broker and a real client story.

How do people get in touch?

The usual answer

A long contact form.

What we did

A direct WhatsApp line, repeated at the end of the page.
