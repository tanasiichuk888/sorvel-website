Work/Alma Secret Tunisie

Beauty · Natural cosmetics · Tunisia

A beauty store built for cash on delivery.

Alma Secret is a Spanish brand of certified natural cosmetics. Its Tunisian distributor needed an online store that works the way Tunisians actually shop: in French, in dinars, paid in cash at the door. We designed the full store and built it on Shopify, from a shop-by-concern homepage to a one-page checkout with no card fields at all.

The challenge

Premium skincare, paid in cash at the door.

In Tunisia most online orders are paid on delivery, and many shoppers never enter a card online. A standard Shopify checkout asks for exactly what they don't want to give, and a courier needs an exact locality to deliver.

At the same time Alma Secret is a premium brand: Ecocert-certified, made in Spain, sold in 24+ countries. The store had to feel as calm and clean as the products, and help people pick the right cream without a beauty advisor next to them.

  • A checkout built for cash on delivery
  • Help people find the right product fast
  • Look like the premium brand it is

No cards, no problem

Card fields scare off shoppers who only pay in cash. The checkout had to drop them completely.

Delivery to a locality

The courier only delivers to an exact locality from its own directory, so the address form had to match it.

Too many choices

Face, body, hair, pregnancy-safe care and packs. Without guidance, people leave before they find their product.

How we worked

Design every screen, then build it on Shopify.

Two clear stages. First every page and state was designed on desktop and mobile, then the store was built on Shopify and checked page by page before launch.

The idea

A checkout people trust.

The order form asks only what a courier needs: name, phone, governorate, locality and address. No account, no card, no second step. The total is shown before the button, and the button says exactly what happens next: you pay when the parcel arrives.

Shop by concern

Start from the problem, not the catalogue.

Right under the hero, a swipeable row of concerns: dry skin, oily skin, anti-aging, damaged hair and more. Each card shows a real face and opens only the products that solve it.

Best-sellers

Proof on every product card.

Each card shows the category, star rating with the number of reviews, price in dinars and a quick add-to-cart button, so people can buy straight from the homepage.

Signature range

Tell the story behind the jar.

The Cleopatra range gets its own block: donkey milk, thermal water, argan and green tea, shown as ingredient chips next to a bright product photo, with one clear link to the whole range.

Trust

Real customers from real cities.

Verified reviews signed with a first name, city and age, from La Marsa to Sousse, followed by the brand's big facts: 30,000+ positive reviews, Ecocert certified and 100% made in Spain.

Mobile

Most orders come from a phone.

Swipeable rows instead of long grids, a product page with the price, stock and both buttons in the first scroll, and a cart that says it plainly: two minutes, no bank card, you pay the courier.

Key decisions

Choices made for the market, not a template.

How do people pay?

The usual answer

The default card checkout.

What we did

A one-page cash-on-delivery form, on the standard Shopify plan.

How does the address work?

The usual answer

Free text fields and a postcode.

What we did

Governorate and locality picked from the courier's own list, so every parcel can be delivered.

How do shoppers choose?

The usual answer

A menu of product types.

What we did

Shop by concern on the homepage, plus a filter drawer on every collection.

What about delivery options?

The usual answer

Several shipping methods to compare.

What we did

One option, 24–72 hours anywhere in Tunisia, free from 150 DT.
