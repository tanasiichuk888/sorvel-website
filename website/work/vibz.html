Work/Vibz

Marketing agency · Short-form video · Switzerland

Short videos, a site with vibe.

Vibz is a Swiss agency that makes short-form videos for brands: script, filming, editing and posting, all from one team. We designed their German-language website in Figma: seven pages on desktop and mobile, built around one action, booking a free consultation.

Vibz · Homepage

The challenge

An agency for TikTok can't look like a law firm.

Vibz sells short, catchy videos for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Their buyers are Swiss business owners and HR teams who still think in print ads and job boards, and need to be convinced that 30-second videos actually bring customers and candidates.

The site had to feel as fast and energetic as the videos, explain three services without long text, back the claims with numbers, and lead every visitor to the same next step: a free consultation.

  • Energy of social media, clarity of a business site
  • Proof that short video works
  • One clear action on every page

A sceptical buyer

Many clients still trust traditional marketing. The site has to show why short video wins, not just say it.

Three services

Short video marketing, social recruiting and corporate video, each with its own buyer and questions.

Mobile first

Their audience lives on phones, so every page needed a mobile layout designed, not squeezed.

How we worked

Homepage first, then every page on two screens.

The homepage set the visual language, then the inner pages followed in the same system, each one designed separately for desktop and mobile.

Structure

What a buyer needs to hear

Problems, services, proof, process, comparison, team and FAQ, in the order a sceptical buyer asks.

  • Site map
  • Homepage story
  • German copy structure

Style

Purple to pink, bold type

A violet-to-magenta gradient from the logo, heavy rounded headlines and soft glows, so the site feels like the feed it sells.

Hero

Mehr Reichweite? Easy!

A big two-colour headline, one line on what Vibz does, the free consultation button and a Google rating. A real person on the right and a growth badge make the promise concrete from the first second.

Problems

Your problems, our solution.

A toggle switches the same three cards between the buyer's problems and how Vibz solves them: low visibility, hard-to-reach audiences and weak engagement. One click turns pain into an offer.

Services

Three services, one panel.

Short video marketing, social recruiting and corporate video sit as tabs. Each shows its key benefits and what Vibz does differently, from full-service production to 7-day delivery.

Proof

Data instead of adjectives.

A chart of monthly users and hours per platform, plus three stat cards on short-form video growth, shows the buyer why the format matters before asking for a meeting.

Comparison

Short video vs. traditional marketing.

A side-by-side list in the brand gradient against a plain white card: cost, engagement, reach, targeting and speed. The difference is visible before you read a word.

Inner pages

The same energy, every page.

About with mission and core values, a portfolio of video case studies, a blog with an article template and a contact page with three ways to reach the team, all ending with the same consultation banner.

Mobile

Designed for the thumb.

Each page has its own mobile layout: stacked hero with the button in reach, swipeable service and team rows, and a full-screen menu with the consultation button always at the bottom.

Homepage design

Key decisions

Choices that turn a scroll into a consultation.

How do you open?

The usual answer

A showreel and a slogan.

What we did

A question the buyer already asks, "Mehr Reichweite?", and a free consultation button.

How do you sell the format?

The usual answer

"Video is the future."

What we did

Platform data, research quotes and a direct comparison with traditional marketing.

How do you show three services?

The usual answer

Three long service pages.

What we did

One tabbed panel with benefits and what makes Vibz different.

What about mobile?

The usual answer

Let the desktop layout stack itself.

What we did

A designed mobile version of every page, plus the menu.
