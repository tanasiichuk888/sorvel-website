Work/WeHike

Travel · Guided hiking tours · Spain

Guided hikes you can book in a minute.

WeHike runs guided hiking trips across Spain, from the Pyrenees to the Sierra Nevada. We designed a website that sparks the urge to go, then shows dates, difficulty and price on every trip so booking is one step away.

Client
WeHike
Industry
Travel, guided tours
Scope
Website design, desktop and mobile
Tools
Figma

The challenge

Inspiration is easy. Booking is where people drop off.

Hiking sites usually have beautiful photos and unclear details. WeHike wanted visitors to see the trips, dates and difficulty straight away.

The audience ranges from beginners to experienced hikers, so every trip needed a clear level.

  • Search from the first screen
  • Dates, level and price on every trip
  • Show the guides' care and safety

Pretty but unclear

Many tour sites hide the practical details behind extra clicks.

Mixed experience levels

Beginners and experienced hikers needed to find the right trip fast.

Safety matters

People want to know who is guiding them and how safe it is.

Key decisions

Small choices that shape the page.

What's on the first screen?

The usual answer

A slideshow and a slogan.

What we did

A headline, a search bar and a real trail photo.

What does a trip card show?

The usual answer

A photo and 'Learn more'.

What we did

Price, dates, level, availability and two actions: details or book.

How do you show trust?

The usual answer

Stock testimonials.

What we did

Named reviews with the trip and date they took part in.
