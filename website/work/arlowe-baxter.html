The challenge

Handing over your home takes a lot of trust.

Landlords choosing a short-let manager are handing a stranger their property, their guests and their income. Most agency sites in this space look the same: stock photos, long lists of services and a contact form at the very bottom.

Arlowe Baxter needed a site that earns trust in seconds, shows the money before the small print, and gets a landlord to the first step, a free income estimate, before anyone picks up the phone.

  • Put the estimate in the first screen, not the last
  • Lead with results landlords care about
  • Feel like a design studio, not a call centre

Every agency looks alike

Stock photos, long service lists and a form at the bottom. Nothing told a landlord why this team was different.

The money was hidden

Landlords care about income first, but the numbers sat deep in the page or behind a call.

The lead came last

The first real action was at the very end. Most visitors never got that far.

Under a month, from Figma to live.

A tight timeline meant clear steps and quick decisions. Each stage ended with something the client could review and approve.

The idea

Sell the outcome, not the service list.

Every section answers one landlord question: how much more will I earn, who looks after my guests, and how do I keep an eye on it. The service list comes after the reason to care.

Hero

An estimate in the first five seconds.

A bright, lived-in home on video, one bold promise and an address-plus-bedrooms form right in the hero. The landlord's first click is the start of a lead, not a scroll to the footer.

Services

Three promises, one fee.

Listing optimisation, guest experience and property care grouped into three clear blocks, with the performance-based fee shown openly beside them. No hunting for the price.

Transparency

Show the dashboard, not just say "reporting".

Owners worry about losing sight of their property. The site shows the owner app in context, with occupancy, cleaning, maintenance, reviews and earnings named as chips you can scan in a glance.

Process

Three steps to hands-off income.

Estimate, onboarding and live performance laid out as one calm path, so the next step is always obvious and every section ends with the same clear call to action.

Look and feel

Warm, editorial, interior-magazine calm.

Soft stone and sage tones, generous photography and a friendly geometric typeface. It feels closer to an interior studio than a lettings agency, which is exactly the point for owners of nice homes.

Key decisions

The choices that made the site sell.

Where does the lead form go?

The usual answer

On a contact page, linked from the footer.

What we did

Address and bedrooms in the hero, so the first click starts an estimate.

How do you talk about fees?

The usual answer

Keep them for the sales call.

What we did

Show the performance-based fee openly, right beside the services.

How do you show reporting?

The usual answer

A bullet point that says "monthly reports".

What we did

Show the owner dashboard itself, with occupancy, cleaning and earnings at a glance.

What should it feel like?

The usual answer

A corporate lettings agency in blue.

What we did

An interior magazine: calm, warm and made for owners of nice homes.
