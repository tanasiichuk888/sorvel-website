Why we still design by hand

Taste is the part
you can't automate.

Anyone can generate a landing page in a minute now. That is exactly why so many of them look the same, and why the ones that feel considered stand out more than ever.

We use modern tools to move fast. But every layout, every line of copy and every detail still goes past a senior designer who knows what to keep and what to cut.

We also keep things simple. No sales calls and no meetings for the sake of meetings. You send a brief, you get a clear plan in writing, and you see real progress every few days. It is a calmer way to run a project, and it shows in the result.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel

Who do you work with best?

Startups, product teams and growing businesses that need senior design and a clean build without hiring a full in-house team. Most of our work is for SaaS, fintech and AI companies, from first launch to a growing product.

Do you only work with tech companies?

No. SaaS, fintech and AI are where we do most of our work, but we also design and build for e-commerce, legal, real estate, automotive and service businesses. See Salinas Defense, College Classics or Arlowe Baxter. If you have a clear goal and a budget for doing it properly, we're a good fit.

Do you design and build, or design only?

Both. We design in Figma and build marketing sites in Webflow, Framer or WordPress. For SaaS products and mobile apps we design the UI and hand your engineers dev-ready files. You can also hire us for design only.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a few days. Send a short brief and you'll get a written plan, timeline and fixed quote within 24 hours.

How does the work run without calls?

Everything happens in writing: a clear brief, a plan with milestones, comments right in Figma and regular progress updates. You review on your own time and every decision is on record.

How is pricing set?

A fixed price per project, agreed before we start and split into milestones. No hourly surprises. Ongoing work can run on a monthly plan.

Who owns the files?

You do. Figma files, the website, the CMS and all assets are yours once the project is complete.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes. We often plug into existing teams, follow your design system and hand work over to your developers, or lead the project end to end if you don't have a team yet.

What happens after launch?

We stay on for fixes, new pages and improvements, either per task or on a monthly plan.
