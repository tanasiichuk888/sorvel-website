About the studio

A small senior team with a lot of reps.

Sorvel is a design and development studio for SaaS, fintech and B2B companies. We started as one designer taking on projects from around the world, and grew into a team that covers design and build end to end.

Our story

From one designer to a senior team.

It began with Mykola designing websites for clients he never met in person, in time zones he had to look up. Teams from all over the world kept coming back, and kept sending their friends.

Over the years the projects grew, from single landing pages to full websites, product interfaces and brand systems. To keep up without lowering the bar, we built a small team of senior designers and developers who share the same standards.

Today we design in Figma and build in Webflow, Framer and WordPress. The way we work has stayed the same: a clear plan in writing, fast progress you can see, and a result you're proud to send to customers.

How we work

Six things we don't compromise on.

Taste over trends

We design what fits your business and your buyers, not what's popular on Dribbble this month.

Written, not spoken

Plans, updates and decisions arrive in writing. You review on your own schedule and nothing gets lost in a call.

Seniors only

The people you meet in the plan are the people who design and build. No juniors learning on your budget.

Small number of projects

We take on a few projects at a time, so each one gets real attention and a fast pace.

Fixed scope, fixed price

You know what you get and what it costs before we start. No surprise invoices.

Built to be handed over

Clean files, a tidy CMS and a short video guide. You should never feel locked in.

The team

People who do the work.

No account managers in the middle. You talk to the designers and developers who build your project.

How we work together

Respect for
your calendar.

Most of our clients run companies. Their days are already full of meetings, and a design project shouldn't add more.

So we work in writing. You get a plan you can forward to your team, updates you can read in two minutes, and a place to leave feedback whenever it suits you. Decisions are recorded, so nothing is "I thought we said".

It sounds small. In practice it's the reason projects move faster and end with fewer surprises.
