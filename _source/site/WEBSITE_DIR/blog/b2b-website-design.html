B2B website design: how to build a site that generates leads

B2B buyers rarely buy on their first visit. They come back several times, often with colleagues, and each time the website has to answer a different question. A good B2B site is built for that whole journey, not just the first impression.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 1 November 2026

The short answer

A B2B website that generates leads makes clear who you help and with what, proves it with case studies and named clients, and gives every type of visitor a next step, from a guide to download to a quote request.

A professional B2B website usually costs $5,000 to $20,000 and takes 4 to 8 weeks. With our studio it starts from $6,900.

Key takeaways

Write for the buying committee, not one person. Make proof specific. Offer two kinds of action: one for buyers who are ready and one for those still researching. Connect every form to your CRM.

How B2B buyers actually decide

A typical B2B purchase involves several people: a user who feels the problem, a manager who owns the budget and often someone from finance, IT or legal. Each of them visits your site with a different question:

WhoTheir questionPage that answers it
UserWill this make my job easier?Product and use cases
ManagerWill it pay off, and who else uses it?Case studies, pricing
Finance or procurementWhat does it cost and what are the terms?Pricing, how we work
IT or legalIs it secure and compliant?Security, FAQ

On a recruitment firm's site we led with the firm's hiring numbers and gave founders and engineers separate paths through the same site, because they arrive with completely different goals.

Messaging that works for B2B

  • Name the customer. "For logistics companies with 50 to 500 drivers" beats "for businesses of all sizes".
  • Name the outcome. Fewer violations, faster hiring, lower cost per lead.
  • Use their words. The terms your buyers use in sales calls, not internal jargon.
  • Show, then tell. A product screen or a process diagram before the paragraph.

Proof beats promises

  • Case studies with specifics: the problem, what was done and what changed.
  • Named logos and quotes from real people with their role and company.
  • Numbers you can stand behind, such as clients served, years in business or response time.
  • Certifications and partners that matter in your industry.

Generic testimonials like "Great team, highly recommended" do little. A quote that mentions a specific result or moment is far more convincing.

Lead capture that works

  • A short contact form: name, email, company and one question. Every extra field costs leads.
  • A softer option for early researchers: a guide, checklist or pricing sheet.
  • A clear response time, for example "reply within one business day".
  • CRM integration, so no lead sits unseen in an inbox.
  • Analytics on every form, so you know which pages bring qualified leads.

Which platform for a B2B website

Webflow is a strong default for marketing teams that publish case studies and articles often and want to edit without developers. WordPress fits when you need complex functionality or many integrations. For a fast, smaller site, Framer works well. See Webflow vs WordPress.

Common B2B website mistakes

  • A homepage that talks about the company instead of the customer.
  • No case studies, or case studies with no details.
  • Only one call to action: "Book a demo", with nothing for early researchers.
  • Forms that ask for budget, company size and phone before saying hello.
  • No clear prices or starting points, so buyers go to a competitor that has them.

How many pages does a B2B website need?

Most start with 6 to 12 pages and grow through case studies, industry pages and articles.

How much does a B2B website cost?

Which platform is best for a B2B website?

Webflow is a strong default for marketing teams; WordPress fits complex functionality.

How long does a B2B website take?

Usually 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the number of layouts and content readiness.

Should we show prices on a B2B website?

Starting prices or ranges help qualify leads and build trust, even if final pricing is custom.

Can you connect forms to our CRM?
