Webflow vs Framer: which one to choose in 2026

We design and build on both Webflow and Framer every month, so we have no reason to push one. Here is the short answer, then the details that should decide it for your team.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 31 October 2026

The short answer

Choose Webflow if your marketing team publishes a lot: a blog, case studies, a changelog, integrations or hundreds of SEO pages. Its CMS, roles and structure scale better as the site grows.

Choose Framer if you need a fast, motion-rich site with fewer content types, and your team wants to edit pages as easily as a design file. It is often quicker to launch.

Rule of thumb

Count your content types, not your pages. One or two content types: Framer is usually enough. Four or more, or a content team that publishes weekly: Webflow.

Both host your site on a fast global network, both handle SSL and forms, and both can reach good Core Web Vitals when the build is clean. The real differences are content structure and how your team will work after launch.

When Webflow is the better choice

  • You plan to grow through content. Blog, guides, comparison pages, glossary, integrations directory. Webflow's CMS handles linked collections and filtering well.
  • Several people edit the site. Roles and a structured editor keep content people away from the layout.
  • SEO is a main channel. You get fine control over redirects, meta per item, schema and clean URL structures.
  • You are moving from WordPress. Webflow is the most common target for content-rich WordPress sites. See how to migrate without losing SEO.

When Framer is the better choice

  • You need to launch quickly. A pre-seed or seed startup, a product launch, a waitlist or a campaign site.
  • Motion is part of the story. Scroll effects, interactive product scenes and polished transitions are faster to build in Framer.
  • Your designer will own the site. Framer feels like a design tool, so the person who designs can also update it.
  • The content is simple. A handful of pages plus a blog fits Framer's CMS without workarounds.

What each one costs

Both platforms charge a monthly site plan, and prices change, so check their current pricing pages. The bigger cost is the design and build. With our studio a website starts from $6,900 and a single landing page from $2,400, on either platform. A Framer build is usually slightly cheaper for the same design; a Webflow build pays back when the site grows through content.

Can you switch later?

Yes, but it is a rebuild, not an export. Pages have to be recreated and the CMS content moved with redirects in place. Picking the right platform first is cheaper than switching a year later, which is why we ask how your team will publish before we recommend one.

Summary

In one minute

Both are excellent. Framer is faster for launch sites and motion. Webflow is stronger for content, SEO at scale and larger teams. Count your content types and editors, and the choice usually becomes obvious.

Editing and collaboration

  • Framer: edit on the canvas like a design file. Quick for designers; non-designers can move things they should not.
  • Webflow: a separate editor for content people, so copy and CMS items change without touching the layout. Better for larger teams.

Speed and SEO in practice

Both platforms host on fast global networks and can reach good Core Web Vitals. In practice, speed depends on image sizes, fonts and how much motion is used. For SEO, both handle titles, descriptions and sitemaps; Webflow gives more control over structured CMS pages, redirects and schema when the site grows into hundreds of pages.

Is Framer better than Webflow for SEO?

For small sites both are fine. For sites that grow through content, Webflow gives more control over CMS structure, redirects and schema, which matters at scale.

Is Framer cheaper than Webflow?

The site plans are in a similar range. A Framer build is often a little cheaper and faster, while Webflow pays back on content-heavy sites.

Which is easier for a marketing team to edit?

Framer is easier for designers and quick text changes. Webflow's editor is safer for larger teams because people edit content without touching the layout.

Can you build on both?

Yes. We design in Figma and build in Webflow or Framer, and recommend one after looking at your content and team.

Should a SaaS startup use Webflow or Framer?

Pre-launch or seed with a few pages: Framer. Series A and later with a blog, changelog and SEO plans: Webflow.

Can I start on Framer and move to Webflow later?

Yes, but it is a rebuild. If you expect to grow content quickly, starting on Webflow saves that cost.

Which is better for a portfolio?

Framer, in most cases, thanks to its motion tools and fast editing.
