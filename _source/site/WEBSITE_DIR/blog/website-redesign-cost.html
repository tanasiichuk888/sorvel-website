How much a website redesign costs in 2026

A redesign is a chance to fix what is not working, not just to change colours. What it costs depends on how much has to change underneath.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 31 October 2026

In this article

The short answerRefresh, redesign or rebuildSigns you need a redesignHow to redesign without losing trafficSummaryWhere a redesign budget goesHow a redesign runsHow to measure success

The short answer

A professional redesign of a small to mid-size business website usually costs $5,000 to $20,000 and takes 3 to 8 weeks. With our studio a full redesign and rebuild starts from $6,900. If only the look needs updating, a lighter refresh or a monthly design plan can cost much less.

RedesignNew structure, messaging and design on the same platform$5,000 to $15,000
Redesign and replatformNew design and a move, for example WordPress to Webflow$7,000 to $25,000

Signs you need a redesign

  • Visitors arrive but rarely enquire or sign up.
  • The site looks older than your product or service.
  • Your team needs a developer for simple changes.
  • Pages load slowly on phones or fail Core Web Vitals.
  • The message no longer matches who you sell to.

How to redesign without losing traffic

  • List the pages that bring visits and links before you change anything.
  • Keep URLs where you can, and 301-redirect every one that changes.
  • Carry over titles, descriptions and structured data.
  • Watch Search Console for a few weeks after launch.

Summary

In one minute

A redesign costs more when the structure, message or platform changes, and less when only the look changes. Protect your SEO with redirects and kept metadata. Measure the current site first, so you know what the redesign must improve.

Where a redesign budget goes

PartTypical shareIncludes
Audit and strategy10 to 15%Analytics review, content audit, goals
Structure and messaging10 to 15%Sitemap, page outlines, key messages
Design30 to 35%Desktop and mobile in Figma, components
Build25 to 35%Pages, CMS, integrations
SEO protection and launch5 to 10%Redirects, metadata, QA, monitoring

How a redesign runs

  1. Audit: what works today, what does not, and which pages bring traffic.
  2. Plan: structure, messaging and the scope, with a fixed price.
  3. Design: key pages first, then the rest on the same components.
  4. Build: on the same platform or a new one.
  5. Launch: redirects, checks and a few weeks of monitoring.

How to measure success

  • Conversion rate on key pages: demo requests, inquiries, sales.
  • Organic traffic and rankings before and after.
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals.
  • Time your team spends on updates.

How long does a website redesign take?

Usually 3 to 8 weeks, depending on the number of layouts, content readiness and whether the platform changes.

Will a redesign hurt my SEO?

Not if URLs are kept or redirected, metadata is carried over and indexing is checked after launch.

Should I redesign or just refresh?

Refresh if the structure and message still work. Redesign if visitors do not convert or the site no longer fits the business.

Can you redesign my site on the same platform?

Yes. We redesign on Webflow, Framer or WordPress, or move you to a better fit if it makes sense.

Should we redesign and change platform at the same time?

Often yes, as it saves building twice. Plan SEO carefully if URLs change.

Can we redesign in stages?

Yes. Start with the homepage and key landing pages, then the rest on the same design system.
