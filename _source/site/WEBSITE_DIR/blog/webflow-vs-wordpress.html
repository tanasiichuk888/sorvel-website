Webflow vs WordPress: which is better for a business website in 2026

We build on both platforms and migrate sites in both directions. For most marketing websites the answer is clear, but there are cases where WordPress is still the better tool.

Mykola TanasiichukFounder, Sorvel · 31 October 2026

The short answer

Choose Webflow for a marketing website that your team edits without a developer: no plugin updates, no security patches, hosting included and clean, fast pages.

Choose WordPress if you need things a website builder does not do well: complex membership or LMS features, very large content libraries with custom logic, deep WooCommerce stores, or full control over your own server.

Rule of thumb

If your site is mainly pages, a blog and forms, Webflow costs less to run. If your site is really an application, WordPress (or custom code) gives you more room.

Why many teams move to Webflow

  • No maintenance work. No plugin conflicts, no core updates breaking the layout, no security patches on a Friday night.
  • Marketing ships without developers. New landing pages and posts are built from the existing components.
  • Design stays consistent. The site keeps looking like the design, instead of drifting with every page builder edit.
  • Good Core Web Vitals. Clean markup and global hosting make fast pages easier to keep.

When WordPress is still the right choice

  • Complex functionality. Memberships, courses, bookings with custom rules, multi-language with many editors, or deep integrations with your own systems.
  • Large stores. WooCommerce handles big catalogues and custom checkout logic better than a website builder.
  • You have a developer on hand. With someone maintaining it, WordPress is flexible and cheap to host.

We also build on WordPress, so if your project needs it, that is what we recommend.

Total cost of ownership

WordPress looks cheaper because hosting can cost a few dollars a month. The real cost is maintenance: updates, plugin licences, security, fixes after updates and developer time for small changes. Webflow costs more per month but usually less per year once that time is counted. Moving an existing site from WordPress to Webflow with us starts from $2,900; see what a migration costs.

Summary

In one minute

For a marketing website your team edits itself, Webflow usually costs less to run and stays faster and more consistent. WordPress is still the right tool for complex functionality, large stores and teams with a developer on hand.

Moving from WordPress to Webflow

A careful migration keeps your rankings: a content audit, a CMS model for posts and categories, content import, a full 301 redirect map, carried-over meta data and checks in Search Console after launch. It usually takes 1 to 3 weeks for a typical company site. Details in how to migrate without losing SEO and what a migration costs.

Quick decision checklist

  • Do you need a store with many products or custom checkout? Choose WordPress or Shopify.
  • Do you need memberships, courses or bookings with custom rules? Choose WordPress.
  • Is your site mainly pages, a blog, case studies and forms? Choose Webflow.
  • Do you want no plugin updates and no security work? Choose Webflow.
  • Do you have a developer who maintains the site? WordPress becomes easier.

Is Webflow better than WordPress for SEO?

Both can rank equally well. Webflow has the SEO basics built in and tends to be faster out of the box; WordPress reaches the same level with good plugins and hosting.

Is Webflow more expensive than WordPress?

Per month, usually yes. Per year, often not, because there is no maintenance, plugin licences or developer time for routine changes.

Can I move from WordPress to Webflow without losing rankings?

Yes, with a content audit, a full 301 redirect map, kept meta data and checks in Search Console after launch.

Is WordPress more secure than Webflow?

WordPress can be secure, but security is your job. Webflow handles hosting, SSL and platform updates for you, so there are no plugins to patch.

Do you build on both?

Yes. We design in Figma and build in Webflow, Framer or WordPress, and recommend the platform after looking at what your site needs to do.

How long does a WordPress to Webflow migration take?

Usually 1 to 3 weeks for a typical company site with a blog.

Can I keep my blog posts when moving to Webflow?

Yes. Posts are imported into the Webflow CMS with their images, categories and metadata.
